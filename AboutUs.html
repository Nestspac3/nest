About us

Our story

We noticed how most parent-teacher communication tools focus on sending occasional announcements rather than keeping families involved in a student's daily progress. As two high school students and a teacher in South Africa, we saw the gap from both sides of the desk—so we decided to build the solution ourselves. Nest is a clean, real-time portal that keeps parents, teachers, and students on the same page every day—not just once in a while.

Our Plan

A great app is useless if half the parents at a school can’t load it because they don’t have enough data or using an older phone. To actually make Nest work in South African schools, we’re focusing on three things:

No more end-of-term surprises

Instant, straightforward updates on marks, attendance, and behavior

Extremely user friendly by design:

Keeping login to the app as simple as requesting an OTP to catter for those who aren’t as comfortable with technology, the app is designed to be lightweight when it comes to internet so it never gets in the way of a parent checking in on their child.

Built for teachers ready to make a change

Because a teacher is part of our team, we know firsthand that if a tool adds extra admin, nobody will use it. Nest will be designed to be fast, and easy for schools to set up without a headache.

Want to Build With Us or contribute?

We’re still early, and we’re building Nest from the ground up. If you’re a developer, designer, educator, or someone who wants to help bring Nest to your school, reach out. We’re always open to teaming up with people who care about fixing real problems.
