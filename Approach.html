Our approach

Great school tools spread when teachers, parents, and students actually see them work in a real classroom. Our expansion strategy starts locally and grows through trust: proving how efficient nest is in one school environment first, then updating it with daily feedback from real families, and growing to neighboring schools and districts.

How it works

Pilot & Refine Locally:

Launch Nest in our own school community first. By testing the platform in real classrooms every day, we can iron out friction points and ensure the system saves teachers time while keeping parents genuinely informed.

Build Data-Light Access:

Roll out lightweight, mobile-friendly optimizations—and pursue zero-rated data partnerships—so that any school can adopt Nest without worrying about whether parents can afford mobile data.

Onboard Neighboring Schools:

Take our proven pilot results directly to nearby high schools and primary schools, offering a smooth, guided setup process for administrators and teachers

Scale Nationwide:

Expand our school network across South Africa while continuously adding smarter insights, language support, and tools shaped by the educators and families using Nest every day.

Our Core Principle:

We don't want to just be like any other educational app,we want to make a difference.Every day our team is doing research on how best we can accomplish our goal and what else we can do to reach those affected by the digital divide

Who it helps

Guardians

Guardians will be able to check in on their childs school performance from anywhere in South Africa thanks to Nest being a zero-rated app

Teachers

Teachers will be able to recommend action based on how a student is performing

Students

When a guardian can actually see what is happening in class day-to-day, they have realistic expectations of what the student is dealing with—leading to fairer, more supportive conversations at home instead of nagging or guesswork.
